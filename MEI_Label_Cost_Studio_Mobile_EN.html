Costing methodology

Warp: total ends × woven pitch × crimp allowance, divided among lanes. Weft: for each colour, colour picks per label × woven width × strands per pick, divided among lanes, with tail and waste allowances. Yarn mass in kg = metres × denier / 9,000,000. Each colour has its own price per kg.

Production: physical insertions/min × 60 × lanes / physical insertions per label × efficiency; rejects are separate. Colour accounting: the sum of colour picks must equal physical picks per label; strands per pick covers multiple parallel strands of the SAME colour. This model does not yet cover multiple independently selected colours in one physical insertion. Depreciation: (investment − residual value) / years, over scheduled hours.

Net selling price: cost per label / (1 − target margin − commission). Excludes VAT. Prices and formulas are preliminary; do not double-count compressor electricity, finishing or labour.
